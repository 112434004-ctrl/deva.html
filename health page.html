💧 Daily Health Tips

A balanced day covers exercise, food, sleep and hydration.
  • 🏃 Exercise: Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate activity, 5 days a week.
  • 🥗 Food: Fill half your plate with vegetables and fruit at every meal.
  • 😴 Sleep: Adults need 7–9 hours of quality sleep each night.
  • 🚰 Hydration: Drink roughly 2–3 litres of water daily, more if active.
